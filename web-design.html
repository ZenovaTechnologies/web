GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might be asking.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. Share your current website and tell us what is not working for you. We will review whether targeted improvements or a new build would better suit the project.

Do I need a logo or a domain before contacting you?

No. Tell us what you already have. We can discuss branding and domain requirements during the consultation, with responsibilities and any additional costs agreed in the quote.

How much will my website cost?

The quote depends on the number of pages, the content and features required, and any ongoing management. A consultation helps us define the scope before giving you a price and timescale.

Can we discuss the project remotely?

Yes. Consultations can take place by phone or video call. Zenova is based in Oldham and works with businesses across Greater Manchester.
