Your enquiry details.

This notice explains how information submitted through our contact and consultation forms is used.

What we collect

Your name, email address, chosen service, message, any business name or phone number you provide, your consent and a request reference. Consultation requests also include your preferred date, time and meeting format.

Why we use it

We use these details to respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation. You are not subscribed to marketing messages.

How your request reaches us

Forms are sent through FormSubmit to info@zenovatechnologies.co.uk. FormSubmit processes submissions and provides a spam check; its documentation states that submissions are retained in its archive for 30 days. Our email provider also processes and stores the messages. See FormSubmit’s privacy policy for its handling of information.

Managing your information

Access to enquiry emails is restricted to authorised people handling your request. To ask about access, correction or removal, email info@zenovatechnologies.co.uk or call 07878 937303. Please do not submit passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

Consultation requests

A preferred time is a request, not a reserved appointment. Zenova will contact you to confirm the details. No calendar invitation is generated automatically.

Browser storage

The site uses browser session storage for request references and local storage to remember your cookie choice. Optional Google Analytics loads only after you accept analytics. FormSubmit and its spam-check providers may process technical information on their own pages when you submit a form.

Optional website analytics

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects usage information such as pages viewed, device/browser details and approximate location to help us understand website use. We do not intentionally send your enquiry text, contact details or request reference to Google Analytics. You can reject or withdraw analytics consent using Cookie settings in the footer. Read our cookie policy for details of storage and Google’s information.
