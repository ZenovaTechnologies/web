GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might be asking.

What is a virtual landline?

A virtual landline is a business telephone number with calls handled through a configured phone service, rather than being tied to one traditional desk-phone line. How calls reach you depends on the service and setup you choose.

Can I keep an existing business number?

It may be possible, depending on the number and providers involved. Give us the details during your consultation so we can check eligibility, the process and any charges before you make changes.

Can different team members answer calls?

We can discuss a setup for multiple users and how calls should be routed. The options depend on the chosen system, devices and service plan.

What do I need for a quote?

It helps to know your current provider, number of users, existing numbers, preferred devices and typical call usage. If you are starting from scratch, we can work through those questions together.
