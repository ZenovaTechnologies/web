GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might be asking.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Explain the task in your own words and show us an example if you can. We will help turn the business requirements into a practical plan.

Can you automate a process that uses spreadsheets?

We can review how the spreadsheets are used and identify whether a small tool or automation would help. Sometimes an improvement to the existing process is enough; the right answer depends on your requirements.

Can software connect to a system I already use?

Potentially. It depends on the access and integration features that the existing system provides. We check those dependencies during scoping before including an integration in the project.

How are cost and delivery time agreed?

They depend on the features, data, integrations and level of ongoing support. We define the scope first, then provide a quote and delivery plan for you to review.
